---
title: Talking About the How
date: '2026-11-21'
description: When the problem is open, attack it yourself first instead of planning how to work as a team.
tags: []
---

I recently experienced a hackathon where the team I was in spent a couple of hours asking “how best should we work as a team? Should we all go research the problem? Shall we come up with a list of the top 10 questions to best ask to tackle this problem?” It made me feel uncomfortable, because I was just itching to build and play around with a product, and iterate, and get feedback. When the problem you’re solving is so open, I feel that productivity comes when people have attacked the problem themselves and come back with their takeaways, otherwise, the search space you navigate seems infinite.

When do you just go for it? That is the question. Jumping into the deep end is really an art. Feels like playing a game sometimes ;)
